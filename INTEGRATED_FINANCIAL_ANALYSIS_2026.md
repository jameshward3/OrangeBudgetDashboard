# City of Orange: Complete Budget Analysis 2025-2026
## Integrated PILOT, Delta, and Financial Impact Assessment

---

## Executive Summary

### Overall Budget Health: STABLE WITH CAUTION ⚠️

The City of Orange's 2026 budget shows **measured growth (+2.44%)** with strategic infrastructure investment powered by successful state and federal grant acquisition (+$6.8M). However, underlying pressures in insurance costs, PILOT program aging, and community services reductions require careful monitoring.

---

## I. INTEGRATED FINANCIAL OVERVIEW

### A. Total Budget Movement

| Category | 2025 | 2026 | Change | % |
|----------|------|------|--------|---|
| **Total Budget** | $92.3M | $94.6M | +$2.25M | +2.44% |
| **Core Operations (CAP)** | $66.3M | $64.5M | -$1.75M | -2.63% |
| **Non-Core Operations** | $5.9M | $11.2M | +$5.33M | +90.58% |
| **Total Revenues** | $23.5M | $30.9M | +$7.36M | +31.26% |
| **PILOTs (component of revenues)** | $2.66M | $2.79M | +$124K | +4.64% |

### B. Revenue Composition Shift

**2025 Revenue Mix:**
- Property Tax: 79.0%
- Grants/Special: 0.6%
- PILOTs: 11.3%
- Other Local: 9.1%

**2026 Revenue Mix (Projected):**
- Property Tax: 70.8%
- Grants/Special: 22.5% ⬆️ (NEW)
- PILOTs: 9.0% ⬇️
- Other Local: 8.6%

**INTERPRETATION:** The city is strategically shifting from property tax dependence to grant-funded infrastructure while maintaining PILOT revenue as stable secondary source.

## II. KEY FINANCIAL IMPACTS BY SOURCE

### A. Grant Revenue Impact (LARGEST CHANGE)

**2025 Grants: $150K → 2026 Grants: $6.95M (+4,531%)**

#### New Grants Acquired:
1. **Highland Ave Train Rehabilitation:** $3.0M (Federal)
2. **Legislative Operating Aid:** $2.05M (State)
3. **NJDEP Urban Parks (Colgate):** $860K (State)
4. **Federal Pedestrian Safety:** $597K (Federal)
5. **Other Expanded Grants:** $440K

**Financial Impact:**
- ✅ No local tax increase required for these projects
- ✅ $6.8M infrastructure investment essentially "free"
- ✅ Demonstrates excellent grant writing/acquisition
- ⚠️ Sustainability question - are these recurring?
- ⚠️ One-time projects may not repeat in 2027

**Recommendation:** Budget 2027 conservatively, anticipating most grants are one-time project funding.

---

### B. PILOT Program Revenue Impact

**2025 PILOTs: $2.66M → 2026 PILOTs: $2.79M (+$124K or +4.64%)**

#### Underlying Changes:

**Portfolio Aging Challenge:**
- 13 of 27 properties declining (-56% of portfolio)
- 4 properties completely discontinued
- 2 major projects collapsed (-$306K): Tony Galento (-91%), L&M Development (-53%)
- Net decline in property base = -$151K (before settlements)

**Offsetting New/Growing Properties:**
- 3 newer properties growing (+$30K total)
- Suggests insufficient new PILOT recruitment to replace phase-outs

**2025 Settlement Masks True Performance:**
- One-time $815K settlement inflated 2025 baseline
- Without settlement: 2025 base = $1.85M
- 2026 represents +50.76% growth on recurring base
- But reported as only +4.64% due to settlement comparison

**2027 Forecast:**
- Without new PILOT agreements: **-10 to -15% likely** (-$280K to -$420K)
- Requires aggressive new PILOT recruitment in 2026

**Financial Impact:**
- ✅ Current revenue solid at $2.79M (9% of total)
- ⚠️ Portfolio aging threatens sustainability
- ⚠️ Large project failures indicate underperformance risk
- 🚨 Urgent: Need 2-3 new PILOT agreements within 24 months

---

### C. Insurance Cost Impact (MAJOR CONCERN)

**2025 Insurance: $22.57M → 2026 Insurance: $23.86M (+$1.29M or +5.69%)**

#### Breakdown:

| Insurance Type | 2025 | 2026 | Change | % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health Insurance | $13.57M | $14.31M | +$735K | +5.42% |
| Workers' Comp | $1.0M | $1.5M | +$500K | **+50.0%** 🚨 |
| Liability/Auto | $880K | $1.0M | +$120K | +13.67% |
| Self-Insurance Run-Out | $7.0M | $7.0M | $0 | 0.0% |

**Workers' Compensation Analysis:**
- **Largest percentage increase across ALL budget items**
- $500K one-year increase = 50% spike
- Possible causes:
  1. Claims experience deteriorating (increased payouts)
  2. Insurance carrier rate increase
  3. Expanded coverage/higher limits
  4. Payroll base increase

**Health Insurance Analysis:**
- +$735K reasonable for healthcare inflation (~5.4%)
- Reflects employee enrollments + premium increases
- Consistent with national trends

**Financial Impact:**
- ✅ Health insurance increase expected and manageable
- 🚨 Workers' comp increase requires immediate investigation
- ❌ Insurance now represents 25.2% of total budget (largest single item)
- ⚠️ If trend continues, becomes unsustainable

**Action Required:** CFO must investigate workers' comp increase - is this permanent or one-time?

## XI. CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATIONS

### Overall Assessment: **STABLE BUT VIGILANT**

The City of Orange presents a **fundamentally sound 2026 budget** with:
- ✅ Measured growth (+2.44%)
- ✅ Strategic infrastructure investment
- ✅ Successful external grant funding
- ✅ Maintained fiscal discipline

However, **underlying challenges** require active management:
- ⚠️ 2027 revenue cliff from grant timing
- ⚠️ PILOT portfolio aging and insufficient replacement
- ⚠️ Insurance cost growth unsustainable long-term
- ⚠️ Community services at reduced capacity

### Top 5 Recommendations

1. **Immediate Investigation (30 days):**
   - Workers' compensation cost spike root cause
   - Tony Galento Plaza PILOT collapse
   - Clarify 2027 grant funding timeline

2. **PILOT Recruitment Strategy (Q2-Q3 2026):**
   - Identify 3-5 candidate properties
   - Target 2-3 new agreements by end of 2026
   - Projected impact: +$250-500K annual recurring revenue

3. **Insurance Cost Management (Ongoing):**
   - Implement wellness programs (health)
   - Risk management improvements (workers' comp)
   - Competitive bid for coverage (general)
   - Target: Limit growth to 3-4% annually

4. **2027 Budget Planning (Start Q3 2026):**
   - Develop conservative grant revenue assumptions
   - Plan alternative funding for projects
   - Propose modest tax increase if needed
   - Prepare community for potential service adjustments

5. **Capital Project Execution (Ongoing):**
   - Leverage grant funding for water/sewer modernization
   - Strong project management and oversight
   - Public communication about infrastructure improvements
