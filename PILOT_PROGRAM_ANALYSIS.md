## Understanding PILOTs

### What is a PILOT?
A **Payment-in-Lieu-of-Taxes (PILOT)** agreement is a negotiated payment made by a property owner/developer to a municipality in place of traditional property taxes. PILOTs are commonly used for:
- Incentive-based redevelopment projects
- Tax-exempt institutional properties
- Mixed-use urban renewal developments
- Community development initiatives

### Why Orange Uses PILOTs
1. **Economic Development Incentive** - Attracts developers/investors to underutilized properties
2. **Revenue Certainty** - Provides guaranteed revenue stream independent of tax assessments
3. **Urban Renewal** - Facilitates redevelopment of brownfields/blighted areas
4. **Tax Base Diversification** - Supplements traditional property tax revenue

## Financial Impact Analysis

### Net PILOT Impact 2025→2026

**Gross Changes:**
- Growing Properties: +$4,396 (minimal growth)
- Declining Properties: -$151,319 (significant decline)
- Phase-Out/Discontinued: -$212,763 (lost agreements)
- One-Time Settlement Effect: -$815,000 (2025 only, not recurring)

**Net PILOT Change (Recurring):**
- **2025 Base (excluding settlement): $1,849,627**
- **2026 Recurring: $2,788,300**
- **Net Change: +$938,673** (this assumes phase-outs offset by new agreements)

However, looking at actual reported figures:
- **2025 Actual:** $2,664,627 (includes settlement)
- **2026 Projected:** $2,788,300 (no settlement)
- **Apparent Change: +$123,673 (+4.64%)**

**Reconciliation:**
- Without 2025 settlement: $2,664,627 - $815,000 = $1,849,627 base
- 2026 projected: $2,788,300
- **True recurring growth: +$938,673** or **+50.76%**

**KEY INSIGHT:** The 4.64% reported growth masks SIGNIFICANT underlying changes:
- Strong recovery with new/maturing projects (+50.76%)
- Offset by phase-outs and project completions
- One-time settlement distorts 2025 comparison
